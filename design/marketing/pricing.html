Questions

The details.

Do I pay per product?

No. Fanwise is designed to hold your entire digital-product catalog. Your subscription does not increase because you add more products.

What counts as a marketplace?

A marketplace is an external sales destination connected to Fanwise, such as Etsy, Creative Market, Framer, or another supported platform.

Does my own store count as a marketplace?

One owned storefront is included with Fanwise. External marketplaces are added for $6 each.

What if I stop selling on a marketplace?

Disconnect it and the additional $6 monthly charge goes away at the next applicable billing period.

Does Fanwise take a percentage of my sales?

No. Fanwise charges a subscription and does not take a percentage of your revenue.

Can I have unlimited products?

Yes, subject to reasonable storage and fair-use limits. Fanwise should become the source of truth for your whole catalog.

What happens if I use multiple accounts on the same marketplace?

Additional connected seller accounts may be billed separately, because each account requires its own publishing, synchronization, and analytics connection.

Is AI usage included?

Normal AI merchandising and listing-generation usage is included within reasonable limits. Very high-volume automation may eventually require a higher plan or an additional usage allowance.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel Fanwise or remove connected marketplaces at any time.
