After publish

Publishing is a moment. Keeping listings right is the job.

Edits fan out too

Change the price, a preview or the description on the master, and every derived listing updates on its own schedule.

Rejections come with fixes

When a shop bounces a draft, Fanwise shows the shop's reason and a suggested fix. Accepting it rebuilds and resubmits that one draft.

Rules stay current

Marketplace specs are versioned. When a shop changes a rule, affected drafts rebuild and you get a note saying which listings moved.

Sales flow back

Revenue from every channel lands in one ledger, so you can see which shops earn their $6.
