# Strategy

Long-range vision. **Context only. Never a work queue.**

Paste the full master plan here. It describes the eventual company: catalog ownership,
cross-channel synchronization, revenue intelligence, and benchmarking. None of it is
buildable now and reading it as a task list is the main way this project could go wrong.

## The progression

1. Make publishing dramatically easier.
2. Become the source of truth for the catalog.
3. Bring the money and performance data back.
4. Help creators make better decisions.

In that order. Publishing is the wedge, not the company.

## What the wedge is not

Fanwise is not a form-filling bot, is not organized around any one marketplace, and does not
treat marketplace records as the source of truth. If a decision would make any of those
three sentences false, it is the wrong decision.

## Naming

Say Publish, not Submit. Channels, not Marketplaces. Product, not Item. Listing for the
channel-specific representation. Publish Everywhere for the central action.

Do not brand the company around uploader, submitter or syndicator. Those describe the
initial mechanism, not the business.
